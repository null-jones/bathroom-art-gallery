# Display

## The gallery

One room, approximately 8 ft east–west by 5 ft north–south, with a 9 ft
ceiling. The details are in `collection/gallery.yaml` and drawn on the home
page map.

| Wall | Length | Features | Hanging space |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| North | 8 ft | Toilet near the west corner; vanity (about 4 ft) beside it, with mirror and vanity light above | Above the toilet only |
| East | 5 ft | Narrow window (about 1 × 4 ft) at the north end; entrance to the south, set slightly back | Very little |
| South | 8 ft | None | **Main hanging wall** |
| West | 5 ft | Narrow window (about 1 × 4 ft) at the north end | About 4 ft south of the window |

The ceiling light and exhaust fan are towards the west.

## Hanging height

Hang every work so that its **centre is 57 in (145 cm) from the floor**,
the common gallery standard for average eye height. Some institutions use
58 in; pick one and use it everywhere. A group of works hung together is
centred as one block.

To find the hook height:

> hook height = 57 in + half the work's height − distance from the top of the frame to the hanging wire at full tension

Where a fixture is in the way (above the toilet), hang the bottom of the work
7–10 in above it and accept a higher centre.

## Spacing

- Keep 3–6 in between works hung as a group; use the same gap along a wall.
- Leave room beside each work for its placard (6 in for a standard placard).
- Record each work's position in its record (`location.wall`, `location.along_ft`)
  and set `position_confirmed: true` once it is hung, so the map is right.

## The room as a gallery

A washroom is a hard environment for art: water, humidity and frequent
temperature changes.

- Keep works away from the sink's splash zone.
- Glaze works on paper, photographs and textiles, and frame them with a mat so
  the glass does not touch the work.
- Run the exhaust fan during and after use to limit humidity.
- Check works regularly for buckling, mould spots or condensation inside the
  glass; move anything that shows them.
