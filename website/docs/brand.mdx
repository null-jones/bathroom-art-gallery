# Brand

## Name

| Use | Form |
| --- | --- |
| Short name: headers, placards, social media | The J&W Collection |
| Full name: credit lines, footers, citations, first mention in running text | The Jones & Wilson Collection |
| Web addresses, file names, repository | `jw-collection` (an ampersand cannot appear in a URL) |

Write "J&W" with an ampersand and no spaces. Never "J.W.", "JW" or "J & W".

## Wordmark

Two marks, both outlined from the fonts by `brand/wordmark/build_wordmark.py`
so they render the same everywhere:

- **Lockup:** THE / J&W / COLLECTION. Website footer, title cards, printed
  matter.
- **Compact mark:** J&W alone. Website header, favicon, QR code centres, video
  watermarks.

Construction (the script is the reference; do not redraw by hand):

- J and W in Libre Caslon Display.
- Ampersand in EB Garamond Medium at 0.57 of the cap size, raised 0.21 em.
  It deliberately overlaps the W (about 0.07 em) and the J (0.015 em).
- THE and COLLECTION in EB Garamond Medium capitals, letter-spaced 0.32 em,
  centred on the cap-height mass of J&W (the J's descender is left out of the
  centring) and set 0.19 em clear of it above and below.

Colourways:

| Background | Letters | Ampersand | THE / COLLECTION | Files |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wall (oxblood) | Paper | Gilt | Gilt | `jw-lockup-wall.svg`, `jw-compact-wall.svg` |
| Paper | Ink | Ink | Accent | `jw-lockup-paper.svg`, `jw-compact-paper.svg` |

Keep clear space around the mark of at least the height of the ampersand. Do
not stretch, recolour outside these colourways, add effects, or set the name in
another typeface in place of the mark.

depths it has a contrast of 1.1:1 against the oxblood wall and would read as a
slightly purple version of it.

bouncing off the walls.

## Type

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark, website headings | Libre Caslon Display 400 | High contrast; never below 24 px. No italic exists. |
| Everything else | EB Garamond 400, 500, 600 and italics | Body text, navigation, placards. Artwork titles in italic. |

Both are under the SIL Open Font License and are self-hosted by the site; the
font files are in `brand/fonts/`.

Website scale: headings 68 / 44 / 34 px Libre Caslon Display; body 20 px EB
Garamond with 1.6 line spacing; small text 16 px; eyebrows 14 px capitals
letter-spaced 0.32 em. Placard sizes are in [Placards](./placards.md).

## Voice and house style

The collection writes like a real museum: plain, accurate, unhurried. Humour,
when there is any, comes from context, never from puns in the copy.

- Canadian spelling: colour, centre, harbour, catalogue.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons.
- Artwork titles in italic: *Sailboats*. Series names in roman: Ocean Soul series.
- Dates: `1974`, `c. 1960s–80s` (lower-case `c.` for circa, en dash for ranges), `Undated`.
- Dimensions: `40.6 × 50.8 cm (16 × 20 in.)`: height before width, centimetres first, the × sign, "in." with a full stop.
- Unknown makers: "Artist unknown". Never guess an attribution; use "Attributed to" only when someone with grounds says so.
- Placeholders never reach print or the public site as guesses: leave a field empty until it is known.
